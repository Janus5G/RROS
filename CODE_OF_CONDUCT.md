# RROS community conduct

Be respectful, patient and precise. Welcome different backgrounds and skill
levels. Critique code and evidence, not people. Explain uncertainty and accept
corrections without personal attacks.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted contact, publication of private
information and pressure to perform unsafe hardware experiments are not
acceptable. Do not misrepresent synthetic output or other projects' images
as RROS evidence. Do not upload secrets or someone else's private logs.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful material, redirect discussions,
temporarily restrict participation or exclude repeated abuse. Decisions should
be proportional, explained privately where practical and open to a respectful
appeal through an existing maintainer channel.

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner through an existing private
GitHub/project channel. Do not post personal details in public issues. No
personal contact address or guaranteed response time is advertised.

These are original project guidelines, not a claim of affiliation with an
external code-of-conduct organization.
